🎓 MASTERCLASS CURRICULUM • PREREQUISITES & OUTCOMES

Vedic Astrology Masterclass: Requirements & Outcomes

“Build a strong foundation in the principles of Vedic Astrology rather than simply learning predictions. Jyotish is approached as a science of observation, timing, tendencies and guidance.”

💻 Prerequisites & System Setup

Minimum Requirement: A PC or laptop with a stable internet connection, webcam and microphone, along with a sincere interest in learning Vedic Astrology. No prior knowledge of Astrology is required.

01. What to Expect from the Course

Build a strong foundation in the principles of Vedic Astrology rather than simply learning predictions.

📜
Kundli Interpretation

Learn to understand and interpret a Kundli step by step.

🪐
Core Vedic Framework

Develop familiarity with Grahas, Rashis, Bhavas, Nakshatras, Panchang and Yogas.

✨
Advanced Precision

Introduction to divisional charts and predictive techniques.

🧭
Directional Science

Learn how Jyotish can be used as a directional science for understanding choices, tendencies and life patterns.

⚖️
Karma & Self-Understanding

Explore the connection between Jyotish, Dharma, Karma and self-understanding.

🪔
Theory & Sacred Mythology

Learn through a combination of theory, practical chart analysis, traditional knowledge and mythological stories.

Develop the right skills required for continued research and practical application of Vedic Astrology in consultations.

02. What This Course Is Not

“It is not a shortcut to becoming an expert astrologer.”

Jyotish is approached as a science of observation, timing, tendencies and guidance, encouraging deeper understanding rather than fear-based prediction.

03. After Completing the Course

🎓 Graduate Competencies & Transformative Mastery

Seekers should have the right knowledge and confidence to read a birth chart, apply fundamental Jyotish principles in decision making, take consultations and design remedies, conduct further research, and continue their journey toward deeper research of Vedic Astrology.
